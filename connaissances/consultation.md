# The consultation

## How the consultation works
The free consultation lasts about 30 minutes with an attorney. It takes place by phone or video by default; an in-person meeting at the Lower Manhattan office can be requested. Consultations are offered every weekday at 10 AM, 2 PM or 4 PM New York time.

## What to prepare for the consultation
It helps to have, if available: the police or accident report number, photos of the scene and of the injuries, the names and phone numbers of witnesses, medical records and bills, the caller's own insurance information, and any letter or message received from an insurance company. Nothing is required to book: a caller without these documents can still have a consultation.

## Changing or cancelling a consultation
A caller can call this line again to move the consultation to another day or time; the assistant can change the booking during the call. To cancel, the caller can call the office during business hours.

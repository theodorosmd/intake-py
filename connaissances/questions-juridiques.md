# Legal questions

## Deadlines, chances of winning and amounts
The intake assistant does not answer legal questions: how long the caller has to file a claim, whether the case can be won, how much it could be worth, or what to say to an insurance company. These questions are answered by the attorney during the free consultation, after reviewing the facts.

## Talking to insurance companies
The intake assistant cannot tell the caller what to do about an insurance company. The caller can bring any letter or message from an insurer to the consultation, where the attorney will review it.

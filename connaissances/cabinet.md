# The firm

## Cases the firm handles
The firm only handles personal injury cases in New York State: car, truck and motorcycle accidents, pedestrian and bicycle accidents, rideshare accidents, slip and fall or trip and fall accidents on someone else's property, construction and workplace accidents, and dog bites.

## Cases the firm does not handle
The firm cannot help with a divorce, a custody or other family law matter, a criminal charge, an immigration case, a bankruptcy, a workers' compensation claim on its own, or a medical malpractice case: it does not handle these cases. The intake assistant cannot recommend another lawyer or firm.

## Office hours and location
The office is open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM New York time, and is located in Lower Manhattan. This intake line is answered at any time, day or night, by the AI assistant. The office is closed on weekends and federal holidays.

## Languages
The attorneys and staff speak English and Spanish. This phone line works in English only. A caller who prefers Spanish can ask for the consultation to be held in Spanish.

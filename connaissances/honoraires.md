# Fees and costs

## How the firm is paid
The firm works on a contingency fee: the client pays no attorney fee unless money is recovered for them, through a settlement or a verdict. There is nothing to pay upfront and nothing to pay to start the case. The fee is a percentage of the amount recovered, usually one third; the exact percentage is set in a written agreement that the attorney explains before anything is signed.

## Price of the consultation
The first consultation is free and comes with no obligation to hire the firm.

## Case expenses
Case expenses, such as filing fees, medical records or expert reports, are paid by the firm while the case is open. How they are handled at the end is explained in the written fee agreement.
